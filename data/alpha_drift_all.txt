====================================================================================================
Interpretation guide
====================================================================================================
  * theta < 1/6 and alpha_eff decreasing towards 0.5 as L grows  ->
    the published deviation is a finite-size correction and the
    renormalisation-group prediction alpha = 1/2 survives.
  * theta < 1/6 and alpha_eff L-independent and > 0.5           ->
    genuine violation of the prediction.
  * theta > 1/6 and alpha_eff decreasing                        ->
    the crossover to the long-range branch lies beyond the
    accessible sizes.

  CAUTION: with only 3-5 size pairs the scatter of the pair slopes is
  comparable to the deviations being tested; see the bootstrap errors
  above before drawing conclusions from any single pair.
